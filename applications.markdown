## Applications of AI in Healthcare

AI is being used in healthcare through predictive and generative applications. Predictive AI, such as Mirai, analyzes mammograms to identify patterns that may indicate a patient's risk of developing breast cancer. Generative AI, such as Abridge, uses patient conversations to create clinical notes and help medical professionals manage documentation.

These technologies can help identify health risks earlier, reduce administrative workloads, and give doctors more time to focus on patients. However, they also raise concerns about inaccurate predictions, patient privacy, data security, and the reliability of AI-generated medical records.
