## Social Concerns 

AI in healthcare can improve patient care, reduce costs, and make medical services more efficient. However, it also raises concerns about unequal access to technology, especially for smaller hospitals and underserved communities.

Another concern is algorithmic bias, as AI systems may produce less accurate results if their training data does not adequately represent different populations. There are also questions about patient privacy, government regulation, and who should be held responsible when AI makes mistakes.

Additionally, overreliance on AI could affect medical professionals' independent judgment and the trust between doctors and patients.
