## Predictive AI in Healthcare


Predictive AI uses machine learning to analyze mammograms and identify patterns that may indicate a patient's risk of developing breast cancer. For example, Mirai can estimate breast cancer risk within the next five years, helping medical professionals identify high-risk patients and recommend earlier or more frequent screenings.

Although this technology can support early detection and improve patient care, inaccurate predictions may cause unnecessary stress, screenings, and medical expenses. Medical professionals should use these predictions as a supporting tool rather than relying on them alone.
